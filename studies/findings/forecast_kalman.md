Generated by `studies/scripts/forecast_kalman.R`. Do not edit by hand.

Gaussian panels of 4 series with a covariate and observation noise; AR(1) and the random walk at times 1 to 40, CAR(1) at 30 irregular times out of 1 to 50. Each series is forecast 1 to 6 steps past its last time, and compared with `stats::KalmanRun()` and `stats::KalmanForecast()` at illume's estimates. limn used `uncertainty = "latent"`, which holds every parameter and draws only the latent cells, with 40000 draws: a draw-mean's Monte Carlo SE is its SD / 200, and an SD's relative error about 0.35%.

**Point forecast and distribution.** The estimate is limn's point forecast, the fitted cells carried forward with no new shocks. The filter uses the residual SD the fit's likelihood uses, exp(logdisp): ar1 0.4530, car1 0.2876, rw1 0.4017. `ilm_varcorr()` reports the same for these fits. (For a gaussian model with nothing integrated out it reports lm's sqrt(RSS / (n - p)) instead, which is not the likelihood's.)

"none" carries the fitted cells forward from their estimates, so it leaves out the uncertainty in the last cell; its ratio is below 1 at short horizons and approaches 1 as the new shocks dominate (for the random walk they never stop adding). "fixed" and "joint" add the uncertainty in the fixed effects and, for "joint", in the variances; the filter has neither.
